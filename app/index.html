      Roadmap to the platinum

      One playthrough is enough — every trophy is single-player and offline, and there are no online trophies. Play the story first, then clean up the map. Expect 200–300 hours in total (PowerPyx, Push Square). The only thing you must do early is Lucia’s Chapter 1 meditation — everything else can wait until free roam after the story.

      Save rule: GTA 6 has no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

        Missable trophies

        Good news first: only one trophy is possibly missable, and it is easy to protect. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story, and nothing on the 100% completion checklist can be lost.

        Save advice that covers everything

        • Manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a save is your only way back.
        • Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 and keep that save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.
        • Keep a save before the final mission, in case you want to replay the ending.
        • Cheats disable trophies for that session (GTABase), so leave cheat codes alone while you hunt.
        • Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent (PowerPyx, GTABase) — read the profile rules before a rampage, though it blocks no trophy by itself.

        About this guide

        Leonida Legend is a free, open-source, offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files — no account, no network, nothing leaves your browser.

        How the tracker works

        • Tick a trophy when it pops. Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) automatically.
        • Export progress downloads a JSON file; Import progress reads it back on any device.
        • Filters narrow the list by kind, grade and earned status; search matches names, descriptions, missions and guide text.
        • Hide story spoilers masks the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
        • Counted trophies (stunts, cards, WAiNK scans…) have a counter in the detail panel — tap + as you go.

        What the data is

        Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it, as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it — 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden. Every guide is written in this entry’s own words and cites its sources beside it. Where no guide has confirmed a method yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.
